# DCM-CMDB Bridge - Monetization Strategy

## Executive Summary

DCM-CMDB Bridge solves a critical enterprise pain: **organizations don't know what's actually running in production**. This "Infrastructure Lie" costs enterprises millions in shadow IT, compliance failures, and security breaches. Our platform exposes the truth with evidence-based governance.

---

## Market Opportunity

### The Problem We Solve
| Pain Point | Cost to Enterprise |
|------------|-------------------|
| Shadow IT in production | $500K-$5M/year in untracked resources |
| CMDB inaccuracy | 60-80% of CMDB records are stale/wrong |
| Compliance failures | $100K-$10M per audit finding |
| Security blind spots | Average breach cost: $4.45M |
| Manual reconciliation | 2-5 FTEs dedicated to CMDB maintenance |
